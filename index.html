ASR removes friction sellers face today — at every stage

New sellers drop out because they can't figure out sourcing. Existing sellers get hit with enforcements from unknowingly bad supply chains. ASR fixes both — by shifting verification upstream to the supplier, before inventory ever enters the store.

The Problem ASR Solves
🚨 New Seller Cold-Start Problem
New sellers spend weeks navigating sourcing decisions with no reliable way to identify verified suppliers. They often fall victim to bad actors or can't find how to fast-track products into Amazon's supply chain. Many abandon selling entirely before their first listing goes live.
🚨 Good Sellers, Bad Supply Chains
Legitimate sellers face enforcements from unknowingly sourcing from bad suppliers — counterfeit risks, authenticity complaints, and account suspensions they didn't cause. With no entity-level view of suppliers, Amazon can't detect supplier switching or contamination until after harm is done.
🚨 Verification Burden at Massive Scale
Hundreds of millions of listing approvals occur across the store. Each one can trigger supply chain verification — invoice uploads, gating reviews, and re-verification cycles that add days of delay per ASIN. The cumulative friction across that volume is enormous, and it falls entirely on sellers today.

How ASR Works — Core Tenets
Tenet 1
We verify the supplier, so sellers don't have to
We proactively verify supplier supply chains through our infrastructure and make the outcome visible. A supplier in the registry means Amazon has done the work — sellers source with confidence, not guesswork.
Tenet 2
We offer choice, not a requirement
Sellers can pick a verified supplier from the registry or bring their own for verification. Both pathways lead to the same outcome: a verified supply chain and a streamlined path to selling. ASR is an available option that removes friction — sellers who use it benefit from faster launch and less documentation overhead.
Tenet 3
We earn trust continuously, not once
A supplier verified at enrollment but sourcing from a counterfeiter six months later loses registry status. We continuously monitor for supply chain deviations. "Registered" always means currently verified.
Tenet 4
We shift verification upstream
Every verification completed at the supplier level is one less verification a seller faces at listing. We move the cost of verification to where it belongs — before inventory enters our store, not after a seller tries to list.
Tenet 5
Suppliers fund the registry, sellers pay nothing
Suppliers pay a low-cost subscription to maintain registry status — gaining distribution reach and access to Amazon Supply Chain Services (ASCS). Sellers pay nothing additional to source from registered suppliers.
Tenet 6
The registry is self-reinforcing
Suppliers enroll for distribution reach. Sellers source to eliminate friction. Amazon benefits from verified supply chains that reduce enforcement cost. Every participant's incentive reinforces the others.

The Full Seller Journey — End to End
📝 New seller begins account registration
At the Supply Chain step of registration, the seller is surfaced ASR as a way to skip supply chain verification and go live faster. This is the primary discovery point — no prior knowledge of ASR needed.
🔍 Seller browses ASR — finds a verified supplier
Inside the ASR marketplace in Seller Central, the seller filters by category and supplier type (Authorized Distributor or Direct Supplier), reads profiles, and selects a match. Amazon has already done the verification.
📨 Seller connects with supplier — requests a quote
With one click, the seller contacts the supplier. The connection is logged in Seller Central, creating a traceable sourcing record. Pricing, lead times, and samples are negotiated directly.
📦 Inventory sourced — routed through ASCS
Registered suppliers route inventory through Amazon Supply Chain Services for end-to-end custody-chain visibility. Amazon tracks the inventory journey from supplier to fulfillment center, creating a verifiable chain of custody.
✅ Listing goes live — supply chain pre-cleared, zero friction
Because the supplier is ASR-registered and the sourcing relationship is on file, the ASIN bypasses offer-stage gating entirely. No invoice to upload, no review wait. The listing is approved and the seller's account health stays green.
🔄 Continuous monitoring protects sellers going forward
ASR continuously monitors registered suppliers for supply chain deviations, contamination, and sourcing changes. If a supplier's status changes, sellers are alerted before it affects their listings — proactive protection, not reactive enforcement.
Amazon Supplier Registry — the supply chain unlock at listing scale
With hundreds of millions of listing approvals occurring across the store, shifting verification upstream to the supplier level — rather than at each individual listing — is where the most significant reduction in friction lies. Sellers who choose ASR benefit from that shift directly.
