Amazon Supplier Registry (ASR)
A supplier discovery and verification platform built into Seller Central. Amazon verifies the supplier — so sellers don't have to. Sourcing from an ASR supplier clears supply chain verification automatically, at every listing.
How it works
🔍
1. Seller discovers ASR
Surfaces at registration (Step 4 — sourcing question) or inside Seller Central during listing creation or an enforcement action.
🏭
2. Seller picks a verified supplier
Browse Authorized Distributors and Direct Suppliers — pre-verified by Amazon. Filter by category, type, location. Connect with one click.
✅
3. Supply chain is pre-cleared
No invoice upload. No gating review wait. The ASIN goes live immediately. The same pre-clearance applies to every future listing from that supplier.

The Impact — What changes when verification moves upstream
ASR shifts supply chain verification from the listing level to the supplier level. At hundreds of millions of listing approvals per year, that shift is the real unlock.
Day 1
New sellers sourcing via ASR can launch a buyable offer on day one — no verification wait, no cold-start delay
100M+
Listing approvals per year that can bypass individual supply chain verification when sourced through ASR
Zero
Invoice uploads required for listings sourced from ASR-registered suppliers — verification is pre-done at the supplier level
Before vs. After
❌ Without ASR — Today
📄
Invoice upload per ASIN — every listing can trigger a manual supply chain review
⏳
Days of gating delay — sellers wait for review before going live, sometimes abandoning Amazon
🔍
No verified supplier discovery — sellers use Google and trade fairs with no quality signal
⚠️
Good sellers harmed by bad suppliers — unknowingly sourcing from bad actors leads to enforcement
👁️
One-time vetting only — a supplier verified once is assumed safe forever, even if their sourcing changes
✅ With ASR
🚫
No invoice uploads — ASR verification at the supplier level auto-clears every listing from that supplier
⚡
Instant listing approval — sellers who source through ASR can bypass offer-stage gating entirely
🏭
Curated verified discovery — browse Authorized Distributors and Direct Suppliers inside Seller Central
🛡️
Proactive protection — ASR flags supplier deviations before they cause seller harm, not after
🔄
Continuous monitoring — "Registered" means currently verified, not verified once and assumed good forever
Who gets what
🏭
Suppliers — pay a subscription, gain distribution
Low-cost subscription to enroll → visibility to Amazon's full seller base as a trusted sourcing option → access to Amazon Supply Chain Services (ASCS) for warehousing and delivery.
Suppliers fund the registry
🛒
Sellers — free access, less friction
Free for all professional-tier sellers → no invoice uploads → faster listing approvals → protection from bad supply chains → optional end-to-end sourcing via ASCS.
Sellers pay nothing extra
ASR — the supply chain unlock at listing scale
