The Seller Journey — End to End
🛒 Seller wants to list a new product
Inside Seller Central's listing creation workflow, the seller is prompted to confirm their supply chain. A banner surfaces Supplier Network Hub as the easiest path forward.
🔍 Seller browses verified suppliers
The seller filters by category, reads supplier profiles — ratings, MOQ, certifications — and finds a match. All suppliers are Amazon-assessed, removing all guesswork.
📨 Seller requests a quote
With one click the seller contacts the supplier directly. Pricing, lead times, and samples are discussed. The connection is logged inside Seller Central for traceability.
📦 Inventory sourced & fulfilled
Goods are shipped to the seller or directly to Amazon FBA. Because the supplier is pre-verified, the supply chain record is already on file with Amazon.
✅ Listing goes live — zero compliance friction
No invoice to upload. No document review wait. No authenticity risk. The listing is approved instantly and the seller's account health stays green.
